# Open a modal window

A new form window is `FormDialog` (`frontend/app/components/ui/FormDialog.tsx`): it portals to `document.body`, joins the modal stack through `useModalLayer`, and has the phone shape built in. A window you draw by hand needs both pieces itself: `createPortal(…, document.body)` and `useModalLayer` spread on its overlay. A yes/no question is `useConfirm` (`.howto/ask-are-you-sure.md`).

## How

- `FormDialog` props: `title`, `onClose`, `tone` (`blue` / `emerald` / `rose`), `size` (`compact` / `form` / `standard` / `wide` — the width comes from here), `dismissOnBackdrop`, `closeDisabled` (a save is in flight), `returnFocusTo` (the surviving control when a temporary opener disappears), `unsaved` (close policy asks before discarding work), `onSubmit` to wrap it in a form. `FormActions` is the Cancel/Save pair.
- Passing `footer` switches the shape: head and foot stay put, only the middle band scrolls, and on a phone the window rises from the bottom edge (`max-h-[92vh]`). Without `footer` it is one scrolling panel, full height on a phone (`h-[100dvh]`).
- A form dialog puts its `FormActions` in `footer` and its submit handler in `onSubmit` (example: `components/calendar/EnginePreachDateModal.tsx`), with `showCloseButton` to keep the X. `FormActions` inside the children scrolls away with long content — twelve dialogs did that from 2026-09-07 until 2026-09-29. Never nest a `<form>` inside the dialog's own form.
- Hand-drawn overlay: `const layer = useModalLayer({ onClose, active: isOpen, closeDisabled })`, then `<div {...layer} className="fixed inset-0 z-[100] …">`. The hook (`frontend/app/hooks/useModalLayer.ts`) holds the page still (counted locks; iOS is pinned by `useScrollLock`, because `overflow: hidden` on the body does not stop Safari; elsewhere the root `html` alone holds it — hidden overflow on the body too makes the body a scroll box, the sticky nav and sticky bars drop to the top of the page under the dialog, and focus given back to a button in them scrolls the reader there, 2026-10-14), lets Escape close only the topmost window, and registers the layer. The backdrop click stays the caller's choice — a form with typing in it must not vanish on a stray tap.
- A hand-drawn window keeps the shared `layer.ref` on its registered element: `useModalLayer` owns registration, initial focus, Tab containment and return focus. If only an inner panel owns the keyboard, pass `focusRing: panelRef`; the registry focuses and returns inside that ring too, and adopts a panel mounted later without re-registering the window. Do not add a caller-owned trap or focus-restoration copy. Main added a direct trap to `BiblePicker.tsx` on2026-10-23; the local intake keeps its new search/picker behavior through the existing shared layer instead. See the Keyboard focus section and the Unit173 reconciliation of BUG-20261023-windows-let-tab-out in `docs/verification/local-bug-fixes.md`.
- Always `createPortal(content, document.body)`. Rendered in the tree, a modal lives inside its ancestors' stacking context (an element that has its own z-order bubble; nothing inside can rise above that element's siblings), so `z-50` still loses to the sticky nav (`z-40`), cards and floating buttons, worst on phones. Guard the server render: return `null` while `typeof document === 'undefined'` (`ConfirmModal`) or until a `mounted` state is set.
- Layers in use: `FormDialog` `z-[100]`, `FormDialog` with footer `z-[110]`, full-screen editors and conduct screens `z-[200]`, `ConfirmModal` `z-[300]` above all.
- The panel carries `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, `aria-labelledby`. `aria-modal="true"` is also the switch `frontend/app/globals.css` keys on to lock the study page's own scroll panes (`[data-study-workspace]`, `[data-note-scroll-region]`) while any dialog is open. The study mobile sheet is marked `data-note-sheet` and sets `aria-modal=false` while mounted but closed; its `data-note-sheet-scroll` pane locks too when another dialog opens above it.
- Focus comes and goes with the window (`useModalLayer`): on showing it moves to the window itself (`[role="dialog"]`, given `tabindex="-1"`) unless a field inside took it (`autoFocus`) — a field taken by the window would raise a phone's keyboard; on closing it goes back to what had it as the window came, read while the window renders (by its effects an `autoFocus` field already has it). From the window itself Tab walks on and Shift+Tab turns round to the window's last control (`useFocusTrap`). Tests: `__tests__/hooks/modalFocus.test.tsx`.
- A window with unsaved typing: `FormDialog unsaved` — the registry's single modal-history owner routes Back through the same guarded `onClose`; if the person keeps editing, the registry arms the next Back entry. Do not add `useBackAsksFirst` beside it. A URL-bearing screen with its own Back owner normally sets `browserHistory: false` on `useModalLayer`. A new analysis instead guards its existing visit with `browserHistory: 'current'` for that entire opening, returning to the same address before asking its departure policy; this reuses `modalHistory`, with no second stack. See `.howto/make-back-close-a-screen.md`.
- A reader panel kept mounted to preserve its scroll position must be `inert` while inactive. `useFocusTrap` excludes inert elements and their descendants from its ring, so Tab and Shift+Tab never try to focus the hidden panel.
- One scroll container per window. No `overflow-y-auto` on small sub-containers inside a phone modal: touch gets trapped in the small box and the rest of the content cannot be reached. The window's content itself must stay scrollable.
- Mount one modal at the container, not one per list item: `Column.tsx` renders `DeletePointConfirmModal` once, and each `OutlinePointCard` asks for it with `onDeletePoint(id)`.
- Several older overlays still render in the tree without a portal (`ColorPickerModal`, `KeyFragmentsModal`, `AudioExportModal`, …) — do not copy them.
- A textarea that grows inside a modal: `.howto/make-a-text-field-grow.md`.

## Bible picker viewport geometry

- `components/bible/BiblePicker.tsx` keeps its anchor measured before `useModalLayer` takes the page scroll lock. Do not remeasure that trigger on resize: a pinned page can move it relative to the viewport. While a wide, noncentered picker is open, its bounded resize subscription updates only viewport dimensions; it is removed on close/unmount.

## Keyboard focus

- `useModalLayer` owns initial focus, Tab/Shift+Tab containment, and return focus. Do not add another Tab listener or generic focus-restoration effect to a modal.
- An existing `autoFocus` or a caller's specific focus effect keeps its chosen control. `ConfirmModal` starts on Cancel; an editor may consume Tab with `preventDefault()` before the shared navigation handler runs.
- If opening from a menu removes the original control, pass `returnFocusTo` (a ref to the surviving menu button). The shared layer also remembers the prior active element as a fallback if that explicit target becomes disabled or disappears.
- Closed but mounted panels must not advertise an active modal: `useModalLayer` removes `data-modal-layer` while inactive, so page gestures and workspace keyboards resume. A positioned popup must also wait until its panel is visible before activation: `VerseMenu` passes `active: spot !== null`. Registering its hidden ring early makes native focus fail, and retaining that same ring does not retry initial focus when visibility changes. Keep initial and return focus with the shared layer, without a caller-owned copy.
- The overlay ref can first appear on a later render. Registration follows the actual element; a stable overlay is not registered again during typing. Reopening captures a new return target.
- Modal keyboard order follows the overlay's computed z-index, then opening order for equal layers. This also handles a confirmation already open when its parent form first mounts. Keep visual layers consistent with the intended foreground window.
- `utils/focusableElements.ts` owns the Tab sequence: available visible controls, no negative tabindex, one checked/first stop per named radio group, positive tabindex ordering. An empty/busy dialog receives programmatic focus on its panel; focus changes use `preventScroll`.
- Headless UI dialogs keep their own trap. The shared mechanism yields when focus is in an external `aria-modal` dialog; custom portals are separate body roots and are not necessarily made inert by that library. Keep the real Headless UI integration regression.
- A full-screen workspace can use `useFocusTrap` directly; it yields while a modal stands above it. Server writes and offline queues are unrelated to focus ownership.
- Verify actual browser Tab and Shift+Tab at both edges, Escape through nested layers, the opener after closing, and page scroll before/after. jsdom checks alone do not prove the browser's native Tab order or physical iPad behavior.

## Traps

- Once `useModalLayer` accepts Escape for an eligible top layer, it calls native `stopImmediatePropagation()` before `onClose`. Ordinary `stopPropagation()` still lets later listeners on the same `window` run. A child close can unregister the layer before a later parent listener, exposing that parent to the same key. Keep the existing active/disabled/topmost/available/external-dialog checks before consumption; keep this rule in the shared hook. Do not replace it with timers, per-window flags or `defaultPrevented`. Earlier document listeners still need their own guards. Regression: `__tests__/hooks/useModalLayer.escapeDispatch.test.tsx`; actual comment and confirmation browser proof: Unit227.

- Background Escape actions yield while `hasOpenLayer()` from `@/hooks/useModalLayer` is true. The reader's phone tool button and verse-copy selection listen on `document`, before the modal's window listener; a later stop cannot undo their earlier action. A toolbar or undo shortcut inside an active registered window asks `keyboardReaches(element)` from `@/utils/modalLayerRegistry`: the element must belong to the top window, and focus must not be inside a newer external modal. With no registered window the query still yields to the broader `hasOpenLayer()` surfaces. Keep this query with the existing registry; do not create another stack. An eligible toolbar Escape stops propagation before putting its tool away, leaving a separate Escape to close its parent. Regression: `__tests__/hooks/modalLayerKeyboardScope.test.tsx`; source composition and native analysis/comment controls: Unit229. The generic `frontend/hooks/useKeyboardShortcut.ts` listens on `window` and uses the same guard; if it runs after an eligible modal, that modal has already consumed the key. Keep background actions unchanged outside windows. Closing a translation picker must leave the selected marker in hand; the next Escape puts it away (BUG-20261027-escape-closes-window-and-tool). This is distinct from an inner control consuming Escape below.
- A part inside a window that answers Escape itself (inline edit cancel, a calendar popover, a keyboard drag lift, a point note) must stop the press: `e.stopPropagation()` in a React handler, or in a document-level listener — the window listens at the window and runs last. Without it one Escape cancels the edit AND closes the window (BUG-20261003-escape-cancelling-inner-action-closes-dialog). Do not key the window on `defaultPrevented` instead: ProseMirror prevents every Escape it sees, so rich-text fields would stop closing their window (`__tests__/components/ui/escapeInsideDialogs.test.tsx`).
- The window itself does not answer Escape a second time in its own `onKeyDown`: `useModalLayer` already closes it. Closing inside React as well closes twice, and the page under it can re-enable its own shortcuts mid-press — the same Escape then reaches them (the time pickers' Escape also left the preaching view; `CustomTimePicker`, `SectionTimePicker`, `__tests__/components/timePickerEnter.test.tsx`). jsdom cannot show that second effect: the browser re-renders between listeners of one press, jsdom does not.

- Study page, wheel scrolling: `useNoteScrollIsolation` scrolls panes with `scrollBy`, and a programmatic scroll ignores `overflow: hidden` — so it checks the lock before scrolling. `useNoteScrollPosition` decides who owns the scroll from the CSS flag `--note-pane-scroll`, never from `overflow`, because a modal changes overflow without changing the owner. Regression to keep: rotating the device repeatedly while a dialog is open must keep the reading anchor.
- Tests: `screen` queries see a portal in `document.body`. Tests that need it inline mock `react-dom` with `...jest.requireActual('react-dom')` plus `createPortal: (node) => node` (`frontend/__tests__/components/navigation/FeedbackModal.test.tsx`).

Inline nonmodal controls belong to the window's shared ring; a child handler can claim a key with `preventDefault`. The external-surface exception applies to separate/portalled surfaces outside the ring, rather than every inline menu role.

A full-screen workspace using `useFocusTrap` yields to `hasModalLayers()` in the canonical registry, not the broad DOM/scroll-lock query `hasOpenLayer()`: its own `aria-modal` identifies the screen, not another window over it. Keep the broad query for background page gestures.
- **A window that steps aside while the page under it is used for its own sake** (a comment's verses chosen in the reader, case 2026-10-12-block-diagram phase 9): `FormDialog` `away` — `useModalLayer({ active: false })` (no held page, no Escape, no Tab ring) and the layer `hidden` plus the class `hidden` (Tailwind 3: the attribute alone loses to `flex`); the window stays mounted with what was typed. `hasOpenLayer` skips everything inside a `[hidden]` ancestor — without that, the window's own `aria-modal` dialog (whose display is its own) still counted as open and Escape did not reach the page (`mineInReader.test.tsx` «changes a written comment's verses»).

## Why

- 2026-02-26: long modals with an inner `max-h` scroll box trapped touch on phones; the fix was one scroll container for the whole window.
- 2026-03-15: modals rendered in the tree sat under the nav and cards on phones despite `z-50`.
- 2026-09-05: a portalled modal over a study must lock every background scroll owner, not just `body`, and the rotation anchor must not mistake that lock for a change of owner.
- 2026-09-16: 38 hand-drawn overlays each decided alone what a modal owes; the owner scrolled his sermon list away behind the open "New sermon" form. `useModalLayer` moved the rules into one hook without rewriting the markup.

See also: `.howto/ask-are-you-sure.md` · `.howto/make-a-text-field-grow.md`


## Browser Back and window close policy

- `useModalLayer` connects browser history to the same current `onClose` and `closeDisabled` policy used by the form. The canonical registry still chooses the visual top by z-index/open order. Do not add a screen-owned popstate listener or another modal stack. Hidden/away layers release ownership.
- `utils/modalHistory.client.ts` owns the transient base/page pair. A consumed traversal returns to the existing page entry before invoking close; it does not push a replacement on pop. Obsolete bases are skipped when pages own Back/Forward again. Unmount never initiates asynchronous Back, which could rewind a newer route. A new unguarded modal is itself a push and can clear old Forward history; a guarded visit is reused.
- Root `WindowHistoryPosition` installs the shared owner; its pop handler delegates before measuring a traversal. Transient copies retain the established visit identity and bypass separate scroll bookkeeping. Preserve opaque Next state. Applications choosing a back-or-fallback action use `canGoBackToPage()`, not raw history.length: a direct entry can contain a modal copy.
- Headless UI dialogs attach the callback ref from `useExternalModalHistory({active,onClose,closeDisabled})` to their actual Dialog root. The library keeps its focus trap/scroll lock; the shared registry adds history ordering only. Ref attachment can happen after the parent layout effect, so use the callback ref rather than assuming a first-render object ref is populated.
- `location.hash` can create a native history entry outside pushState. FoldableMarkdown's hidden-target reveal is an actual case. The shared owner synchronizes that fragment without treating the reveal as close, retaining the page's opaque router context. Native navigation indices are read when available; older browsers retain the guarded one-step path. Never use go(0) for compensation: it reloads the page.
- Local proof: Unit259 in `docs/verification/local-bug-fixes.md`; original Chrome dirty-comment failure, wide/narrow discard/Cancel/Back/Forward/reload, and actual nested ReaderSheet fragment reveal. Component tests cover disabled, inactive, callback refresh and real Headless UI integration separately. Physical gestures and a phone keyboard require their own acceptance.
- Mobile Studies detail: the title opens NoteMobileSheet; its reference Chip is not interactable while that panel is closed. A header title and the Chip can share an accessible name. Open the actual properties sheet before clicking the Chip; do not force-click retained offscreen controls.


## Editable admin drawer

`admin/useAdminUserEditorClose.tsx` owns the field signature, per-opening baseline, shared discard question and accepted submission checkpoint. The drawer sends X, backdrop and Cancel to that same callback; `useModalLayer` already brings Escape and browser Back to it. Keep one close policy rather than one question per button.

Capture the submitted signature before awaiting the real API response. Advance the baseline only for the same still-open session after success; a rejected save preserves the typed values and old baseline. Save intentionally leaves this drawer open with its existing toast. A later clean close is quiet. Promotion-only hidden fields count only in explicit Set mode; manual UID changes retain the existing privilege-reset rule.

During submission, the shared layer receives `closeDisabled`; X/backdrop and the native form fieldset are disabled. `fieldset.contents` preserves the existing flex geometry while disabling nested form controls and Cancel. Keep the immediate submit guard too. Do not add a browser-history listener, queue or second dialog stack here.

Local proof: Unit261 in `docs/verification/local-bug-fixes.md`; real dirty-close baseline, six behavioral REDs, and ordinary Chrome wide/narrow dirty/Keep/Discard/history plus a held original request, actual API200, canonical readback, accepted-save quiet close and effective-counter restore. Missing raw entitlement fields are normalized by the real admin API; do not require defaults to exist in the raw profile. Heartbeat and usage-period metadata are recorded, never rewound to restore a fixture.
