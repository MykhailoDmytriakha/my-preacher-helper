# Show the person's material on the Bible text

Reading a book, every note, sermon, group block and service-order step that names one of its verses stands there as a badge in its section's colour and icon, saying the verses it holds ("9–11"); what names a whole chapter stands by the chapter's heading; the minimap puts a short pill of each kind's colour on its right edge at the height of that verse (a whole chapter's at its label) — the owner's choice 2026-10-21 on the canvas, «на правом краю», a little longer than tall (`minimapMarkRects` in `minimapLayout.ts`, painted by `BibleMinimap`). Pressed, a badge lists what holds that verse and what names its chapter, and a row goes to that document. Nothing is stored: the book gathers what points at it (the owner's board, 2026-10-21, case 2026-10-12-block-diagram, 2.2; the canvas «Перекрёстные ссылки в читалке», row 2.2.3).

## How

- **One reading of where documents keep verses**: `usageDocuments` in `frontend/app/utils/scriptureUsage.ts` — a note's list and its words, a sermon's verse line, plan, thoughts and scratch, a group block's kept passages, words and questions and a meeting's notes (what `groups/conduct/ConductBlock.tsx` shows as links), a service-order step's. Two questions are asked of it: `findScriptureUsage` (who uses this one passage — the verse window's «Мои заметки») and `bookUsage` (everything in this book, verse by verse, in the numbering of the Bible being read through `passagesIn`). A new kind or field is added there once and both see it.
- **A document stands on a place once**: a passage wholly inside another passage of the same document adds nothing (a sermon on 4:9-11 whose thought names 4:10); a badge counts documents, and a list shows each once (a note on chapters 1-2 and 2-3 is one at chapter 2). A reference to a whole book names no place and is left out.
- **Only a place its own Bible has** (`namesAPlace`, `utils/bibleLink.ts`): `passagesIn` falls back to the numbers as written, so a Synodal "Иоил 4:1" (the Synodal ends Joel at 3) stood on Ohienko's real Joel 4:1, and a chapter range up to 999999999 was laid out chapter by chapter (Codex review, 2026-10-21).
- **A badge's words are the place with its chapter** (the owner, 2026-10-21): "3:16", "3:16–18", "3:16–4:18"; by a chapter's heading the chapter itself from its first verse to its last as the reader's Bible numbers it, "3:1–36" — whatever range the document names (`badgeLabel`, `verseCount`).
- **The screen**: `components/bible/useBookUsage.ts` reads the four engine lists (`useDataCollection`), keeps the person's own documents and not one being deleted, and builds the ribbon's badges (`BookRibbon` `mine`, one badge per kind and stretch, "12 · 2" for two; more than three fold into "+N"), the minimap's `mapMarks` and the list (`MineSheet`). A row leaves through `useFollowAfterClose` — the window closes first (iOS pins the page under a window).
- **The badge is one component**, `components/bible/MineBadge.tsx` (the owner's choice 2026-10-21 on the canvas, row 2.2: «Б · Кружок») — after a verse, by a chapter's heading, in Settings → Bible: a light capsule with a thin border, a solid circle of the section's colour with the white icon, the place; the number of documents from 2 as a white circle with a border of the colour on the badge's top right corner, the badge keeping room for it on the right. Its ring shows for the keyboard only (`focus-visible`).
- **Look**: `components/bible/usageKinds.ts` — the menu's section colours and icons (sermons blue, notes green, groups amber, service orders rose); the minimap's canvas takes `dot` colours, light and dark.
- **The switch** is a device choice in Settings → Bible (`minePrefs.ts`, `components/settings/MineSettings.tsx`), not the reader's "T", which is about the text (the owner, 2026-10-20).
- Tests: `__tests__/utils/bookUsage.test.ts`, `__tests__/components/bible/mineInReader.test.tsx`. Live: `.cases/2026-10-12-block-diagram/demo-mine/` (emulator stand, `seed-mine.cjs`, `walk-mine.cjs`).

## Comments (case 2026-10-12-block-diagram, 3.2)

- A comment is a study note with `type: 'comment'` — a few sentences or a short list, like pencil in a margin (the owner, 2026-10-28). `usageDocuments` reads it as its own kind `'comment'` and keeps its words (`body`); it stands **only where it is written to** (its `scriptureRefs`): a verse its words name is a link, not a second place — a note gathers what it names, a comment does not.
- In the reader it is grey words (`components/bible/CommentsOnText.tsx`, `MarkdownDisplay` with `pencil`) under the verse it starts at, inside the verse row (`md:basis-full`, the row is `md:flex-wrap`), so the row's light and choice cover it; a comment on whole chapters stands under **every** heading of them (`RibbonMine.comments.chapters`), never inside a chapter drawn as a box. Settings → Bible → «Комментарии» (`minePrefs.comments`, 'text' by default) turns them into grey badges like the rest; the switch «Значки ваших записей» hides them too.
- Written and edited only in `components/CommentDialog.tsx` (`FormDialog` + `RichMarkdownEditor`, the notes' toolbar): from the verse menu («Комментарий»), from a chapter heading (a button in the heading, `RibbonChapterComment`), from the grey words, from the list behind a badge, from Studies' «Комментарии» tab. A door opens the comment written to that verse or chapter (`useBookUsage().commentOn`), else a new one numbered in the Bible being read.
- **Open at the occurrence the person pressed.** A reference can project into disjoint reader passages: KJV Romans 16:24–27 appears in Synodal 14:24–26 and 16:24. `TextComment.passage`, `MineSheet` and `useCommentDoors` carry the already projected passage into `ReaderCommentDialog`'s transient `{bookId, bible, passage}` opening. `useBookUsage.commentPassage(id, at)` selects from its existing entries with canonical passage coverage; never recalculate numbers in an event handler. A current matching opening supplies the initial title and verse quote. A changed reader book or Bible falls back to its current canonical passage. The edit target remains the stored comment ID; this display context never enters note data.
- **Display is separate from an explicit move.** `CommentDialog.openingPlace` is optional, reader-only initial presentation; `useCommentPlace` remains the single owner of move, Put Back, accepted stored-version display and writes. A real move or taking a stored version supersedes the opening hint; Put Back restores the clicked occurrence. Studies without an opening hint keeps its complete-reference name. Source-Bible coordinates and range length are unchanged until an explicitly saved move. Regression: `mineInReader.test.tsx`, `readerCommentDoors.test.tsx`, `CommentDialog.test.tsx`; native acceptance still checks actual text, badge and keyboard-menu paths.
- The bar «Комментарий → Заметка» measures by `utils/commentLength.ts` (sentences by words, ≈12 a sentence, a list item at least one, more than five — a note); past it the main button «Сделать заметкой» saves the text whole with `type: 'note'`.
- One draft rule for server and device: `utils/studyNoteDraft.ts` — a comment is never a draft (it has no tags). One tab rule for Studies and the note page: `utils/studyNoteTabs.ts`.

## Paragraphs of comments (case 2026-10-12-block-diagram, phase 9)

- A comment on more than one verse is a **paragraph** — nothing new is stored: its `scriptureRefs[0]` spans verses (`toVerse`, and across a chapter's seam `toVerseChapter` — the chapter `toVerse` stands in; `toChapter` beside a verse is read as a leftover of old data, verse wins — `.howto/format-scripture-reference.md`). The rules — never cross, wholly inside or apart, two levels at most — live once in `components/bible/commentParagraphs.ts`: `layParagraphs` for drawing (a comment breaking them, e.g. written in Studies, stays a comment under its first verse, never lost), `fitCommentPassage` for choosing verses.
- When the reader takes another version of a comment, the saved explicit kind wins. For a legacy comment with an accepted reference, infer its kind from that reference projected into the reader's Bible. The clicked occurrence supplies the initial kind and keeps it if the same projected piece remains in the accepted reference. A legacy reference without its own Bible is read through the usual interface-locale default. Taking a version starts a clean edit against it, so Done alone sends no write.
- Drawn by `useBookUsage` (`comments.paragraphs`: headings before the first verse, room after the last) and `BookRibbon` (`RibbonVerse` → `ParagraphHeads`, the heading is `CommentsOnText` itself — one look for a comment's words). The line on the left is `ParagraphLines`: measured from the headings and rows on screen (`data-paragraph-head`, `data-verse`) and measured again by a `ResizeObserver` on the card — reckoning margins failed (collapsing margins, a chapter's heading in between). A comment on one verse stands against the right edge (`CommentsOnText` `align="end"`), so it is not taken for a heading.
- Verses are chosen in the analysis's picker: `useVerseCopy` purpose `'comment'`, the bar `verseCopy/PassagePickBar.tsx`, the rule passed in as `limitComment` (`useCommentLimit`, `ReaderCommentDialog.tsx`). A press inside a passage moves the nearer end (`stretchPassage`), for the analysis too. «Изменить стихи» in the window: the window steps aside (`FormDialog` `away`) and comes back on the bar's «Готово» (`useCommentPlace().moveTo`).
- The verse menu's item is «Комментарий или абзац»; where a comment starts at the verse it says what it opens — «Изменить комментарий» or «Изменить абзац». While verses are chosen (an analysis's or a comment's) the run carries two grips (`verseCopy/PassageGrips.tsx`): pulled with a mouse or a finger, or moved by the arrow keys, an end takes a verse once the pointer is past that verse's middle, down and up alike (`pulledRow`), and goes there under the same rule as a press (`useVerseCopy().moveEnd`, `pulledEnd`); while held, the grip stands under the pointer (one state change a frame, the text scrolled first), let go — on the run's edge; held at the pane's edge the text scrolls on until it can go no further. A pull is followed on the window, not on the grip (a grip whose row leaves the drawn chapters goes, and its release still ends the pull), one pull at a time by its pointer id; a choice let go of ends it. The arrows move an end only to a verse on screen. Letting a grip go is not a press: the click a browser may send after the lift (Safari on iPad, 01.11 — it took the first verse off) is the pull's — the first click on the text after the lift, taken once, before any new press or key there, within half a second (`PULL_CLICK_MS`; `useVerseCopy().pullStarted` / `pullEnded` / `takesPullClick` / `forgetPullClick`; `VerseHoldSurface` `onClickCapture`, `onPointerDown`, `onKeyDownCapture`); a pull during which the text was pressed (a second finger) waits for no click — that press clicks itself. Nothing about the click itself is asked: on the owner's iPad (Pencil) Safari sent it 39 ms after the lift naming another pointer, and a pointer-id match let it through (report, 01.11). A pull the browser broke off (`pointercancel`) waits for no click. A hold is ended only by its own pointer (`useLongPress` keeps the pointer id): a grip's lift no longer finishes a hold a second finger keeps on a verse. Each pull, press, hold and arrow goes into the technical report as one `verse-pick` line — by what (`pull-last`, `press`, `hold`, `arrow-first`, `click` `taken-by-pull`) and how the ends went (`first-later-last-same`), never which verses. One comment window at a time (`useReaderCommenting`): a window stepped aside is never replaced by another — that closed it with its words unasked, and they came back later as «Найден несохранённый текст». And it holds the reader (`useCommentHoldsReader`): another book or translation is a new reader on the page (`bible/page.tsx` keys it by both), so while a window is open those doors bring it back instead, and it asks itself before its words go.
- A comment moved with Move Elsewhere keeps the destination book's paragraph rule. `useBookUsage.paragraphsForBook` projects the already cached owned live notes with canonical `bookUsage` in the reader Bible and the same `layParagraphs`/words lookup; it adds no collection watch. Unknown, failed or read-only note evidence returns no layout, never a proven empty one. `ReaderCommentDialog` checks every projected paragraph piece through `paragraphRefusal`, excluding all pieces of the edited owner. Single verses and whole chapters remain comments without a paragraph check.
- Every verse stands at the same spacing: the translation's own paragraph marks add no room (the paragraphs are the person's).
- Tests: `__tests__/components/bible/commentParagraphs.test.ts`, `mineInReader.test.tsx` («paragraphs of comments»). Live: `.cases/2026-10-12-block-diagram/demo-paragraphs/live-paragraphs.cjs` (writes on the test user and erases what it wrote).

## Traps

- **The minimap has one margin on both sides** (`MINIMAP_INSET`, 4 px): the strokes and labels start that far from the left, the marks end that far from the right, and the reading frame spans the whole width, so the marks stand inside it and the frame keeps the box's 8 px padding on both sides. The frame used to stop 12 px short of the right edge (a scrollbar's room, long hidden): the marks crossed its edge and the map sat closer to the left border than the frame to the right one (the owner, 2026-10-21). Measured by `demo-mine/walk-mine.cjs map`: 8 and 8.

- **The badge stands on the links' chip's line only at its measure**: 20 px high and raised `calc(0.15em + 1px)`. A line holds an inline-flex box by the bottom of its first icon, and the circle's icon ends a pixel higher in the box than the chip's: at 22 px and 0.1em the badge sat 2.6 px lower at the bottom, at 20 px and 0.15em still 1.0 px lower at every text size (the owner's screenshot, 2026-10-21). Measure it, do not look: `demo-mine/walk-mine.cjs align` prints the tops and bottoms on one line at `WALK_SCALE` 0.8–2.

- **Reading a library is slow, so it is read once per version** (measured 2026-10-21): finding references costs about 0.5 ms a page plus 0.05 ms per reference found; the lists come anew with every step of a sync, and 850 documents read again each time took 3.3 s, laid on a book 0.3 s more. `readOnce` keeps each read document by what the reading depends on (the document and the language), `documentInBook` keeps its layout per book and Bible with it: a sync step now costs 3–9 ms. The first reading (about 0.4 s for 420 documents) waits until the text shows: `useDeferredValue(ready)`, React's next pass. Not a timer — set under a test's fake clock, it kept every later test's book from showing (`BibleBookReader.test.tsx`, 33 failures). At most 4000 documents are kept, the oldest read first out.
- **Keyed by value**: the entries go through `JSON.stringify` before they become the ribbon's prop — a new array on each sync step would redraw the whole book's ribbon (`BookRibbon` is `memo`).
- **Badges shape a chapter's height**: the reader's `doubtHeights` effect lists `mine.ribbon`, like the links' chips and the marks' signs.
- **`tr` in the Bible's address is the Bible the place is numbered in, not the one shown**: `/bible?book=psalms&at=23:1&tr=kjv` opens the person's own translation at the same place (Synodal Ps 22:1). To look at King James numbering, switch the translation with its button.
