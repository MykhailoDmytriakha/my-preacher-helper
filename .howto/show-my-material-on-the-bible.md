# Show the person's material on the Bible text

Reading a book, every note, sermon, group block and service-order step that names one of its verses stands there as a badge in its section's colour and icon, saying the verses it holds ("9–11"); what names a whole chapter stands by the chapter's heading; the minimap puts a short pill of each kind's colour on its right edge at the height of that verse (a whole chapter's at its label) — the owner's choice 2026-10-21 on the canvas, «на правом краю», a little longer than tall (`minimapMarkRects` in `minimapLayout.ts`, painted by `BibleMinimap`). Pressed, a badge lists what holds that verse and what names its chapter, and a row goes to that document. Nothing is stored: the book gathers what points at it (the owner's board, 2026-10-21, case 2026-10-12-block-diagram, 2.2; the canvas «Перекрёстные ссылки в читалке», row 2.2.3).

## How

- **One reading of where documents keep verses**: `usageDocuments` in `frontend/app/utils/scriptureUsage.ts` — a note's list and its words, a sermon's verse line, plan, thoughts and scratch, a group block's kept passages, words and questions and a meeting's notes (what `groups/conduct/ConductBlock.tsx` shows as links), a service-order step's. Two questions are asked of it: `findScriptureUsage` (who uses this one passage — the verse window's «Мои заметки») and `bookUsage` (everything in this book, verse by verse, in the numbering of the Bible being read through `passagesIn`). A new kind or field is added there once and both see it.
- **A document stands on a place once**: a passage wholly inside another passage of the same document adds nothing (a sermon on 4:9-11 whose thought names 4:10); a badge counts documents, and a list shows each once (a note on chapters 1-2 and 2-3 is one at chapter 2). A reference to a whole book names no place and is left out.
- **Only a place its own Bible has** (`namesAPlace`, `utils/bibleLink.ts`): `passagesIn` falls back to the numbers as written, so a Synodal "Иоил 4:1" (the Synodal ends Joel at 3) stood on Ohienko's real Joel 4:1, and a chapter range up to 999999999 was laid out chapter by chapter (Codex review, 2026-10-21).
- **A badge's words are the place with its chapter** (the owner, 2026-10-21): "3:16", "3:16–18", "3:16–4:18"; by a chapter's heading the chapter itself from its first verse to its last as the reader's Bible numbers it, "3:1–36" — whatever range the document names (`badgeLabel`, `verseCount`).
- **The screen**: `components/bible/useBookUsage.ts` reads the four engine lists (`useDataCollection`), keeps the person's own documents and not one being deleted, and builds the ribbon's badges (`BookRibbon` `mine`, one badge per kind and stretch, "12 · 2" for two; more than three fold into "+N"), the minimap's `mapMarks` and the list (`MineSheet`). A row leaves through `useFollowAfterClose` — the window closes first (iOS pins the page under a window).
- **The badge is one component**, `components/bible/MineBadge.tsx` (the owner's choice 2026-10-21 on the canvas, row 2.2: «Б · Кружок») — after a verse, by a chapter's heading, in Settings → Bible: a light capsule with a thin border, a solid circle of the section's colour with the white icon, the place; the number of documents from 2 as a white circle with a border of the colour on the badge's top right corner, the badge keeping room for it on the right. Its ring shows for the keyboard only (`focus-visible`).
- **Look**: `components/bible/usageKinds.ts` — the menu's section colours and icons (sermons blue, notes green, groups amber, service orders rose); the minimap's canvas takes `dot` colours, light and dark.
- **The switch** is a device choice in Settings → Bible (`minePrefs.ts`, `components/settings/MineSettings.tsx`), not the reader's "T", which is about the text (the owner, 2026-10-20).
- Tests: `__tests__/utils/bookUsage.test.ts`, `__tests__/components/bible/mineInReader.test.tsx`. Live: `.cases/2026-10-12-block-diagram/demo-mine/` (emulator stand, `seed-mine.cjs`, `walk-mine.cjs`).

## Traps

- **The minimap has one margin on both sides** (`MINIMAP_INSET`, 4 px): the strokes and labels start that far from the left, the marks end that far from the right, and the reading frame spans the whole width, so the marks stand inside it and the frame keeps the box's 8 px padding on both sides. The frame used to stop 12 px short of the right edge (a scrollbar's room, long hidden): the marks crossed its edge and the map sat closer to the left border than the frame to the right one (the owner, 2026-10-21). Measured by `demo-mine/walk-mine.cjs map`: 8 and 8.

- **The badge stands on the links' chip's line only at its measure**: 20 px high and raised `calc(0.15em + 1px)`. A line holds an inline-flex box by the bottom of its first icon, and the circle's icon ends a pixel higher in the box than the chip's: at 22 px and 0.1em the badge sat 2.6 px lower at the bottom, at 20 px and 0.15em still 1.0 px lower at every text size (the owner's screenshot, 2026-10-21). Measure it, do not look: `demo-mine/walk-mine.cjs align` prints the tops and bottoms on one line at `WALK_SCALE` 0.8–2.

- **Reading a library is slow, so it is read once per version** (measured 2026-10-21): finding references costs about 0.5 ms a page plus 0.05 ms per reference found; the lists come anew with every step of a sync, and 850 documents read again each time took 3.3 s, laid on a book 0.3 s more. `readOnce` keeps each read document by what the reading depends on (the document and the language), `documentInBook` keeps its layout per book and Bible with it: a sync step now costs 3–9 ms. The first reading (about 0.4 s for 420 documents) waits until the text shows: `useDeferredValue(ready)`, React's next pass. Not a timer — set under a test's fake clock, it kept every later test's book from showing (`BibleBookReader.test.tsx`, 33 failures). At most 4000 documents are kept, the oldest read first out.
- **Keyed by value**: the entries go through `JSON.stringify` before they become the ribbon's prop — a new array on each sync step would redraw the whole book's ribbon (`BookRibbon` is `memo`).
- **Badges shape a chapter's height**: the reader's `doubtHeights` effect lists `mine.ribbon`, like the links' chips and the marks' signs.
- **`tr` in the Bible's address is the Bible the place is numbered in, not the one shown**: `/bible?book=psalms&at=23:1&tr=kjv` opens the person's own translation at the same place (Synodal Ps 22:1). To look at King James numbering, switch the translation with its button.
