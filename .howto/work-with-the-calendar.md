# Put something on the calendar

Every source is translated once into a `CalendarEntry` in `frontend/app/utils/calendarEntries.ts` (`sermonEntries`, `groupEntries`, `councilEntries`, `noteEntries`, `prayerEntries`); what each kind looks like — icon, dot, legend, date badge, translation keys — is one line of `CALENDAR_KIND_STYLE` in `frontend/app/components/calendar/calendarKinds.tsx`. A new kind is a name in `CALENDAR_KINDS`, a builder and a style line — never a pass through the day cell, the card, the agenda and the filters.

## How

- The calendar speaks one date form: a `YYYY-MM-DD` string. Normalize at the boundary; the server writes preach dates through `toDateOnlyKey` (`frontend/app/api/repositories/sermons.repository.ts`).
- A chosen day (preach date, group meeting, council) → `toDateOnlyKey`: it keeps the stored `YYYY-MM-DD` prefix, which is the day the person picked. A clock stamp (`createdAt`, `updatedAt`, `answeredAt`) → `toLocalDateOnlyKey`, the local day of that instant; otherwise evening work west of UTC lands on tomorrow. Back to a `Date` → `parseDateOnlyAsLocalDate`, never `new Date('YYYY-MM-DD')`, which is UTC midnight. All in `frontend/app/utils/dateOnly.ts`.
- A thing with two stamps (note written/updated, prayer brought/answered) shows one card per day; when both fall on one day the first event keeps it. Only the second appearance carries a status chip (`updated`, `answered`), which says why it is there twice. An answer counts only while the prayer still stands answered.
- Views come from one pipeline: the page concatenates the builders' output, then `entriesByDate`, `kindsByDate`, `entriesInMonth`, `countByKind`. Exception: the analytics view and the planned/preached month summary still reduce `sermon.preachDates` themselves in `frontend/app/(pages)/(private)/calendar/page.tsx` — keep them on `toDateOnlyKey` too.
- The month on screen is not the clicked day: `currentMonth` and `selectedDate` are separate state, and children get `currentMonth` + `onMonthChange` (`PreachCalendar.tsx`, DayPicker `month`).
- A date input goes through `DatePickerField` (`frontend/app/components/ui/DatePickerField.tsx`, value and `onChange` in `YYYY-MM-DD`). It honours the person's week start via `getWeekStartsOn(settings.firstDayOfWeek)` (`frontend/app/utils/weekStart.ts`: sunday by default → 0, monday → 1). A native `input type="date"` popup cannot follow an app-level week start, mobile included.
- `DatePickerField` hands a form every keystroke: its `pattern` refuses a half-typed date at submit, a draft keeps what was typed. A form that creates a record passes `refuseMissingDays` (the new-council form), and the field refuses a day that does not exist (`isMissingDay` → `setCustomValidity`: "2026-02-31", "0000-01-01") as the browser's date field did. An editing form must not: an old record or a recovered draft holding such a day would stop saving its other fields (BUG-20261003-date-form-accepts-impossible-day).
- A caller that saves on every `onChange` and for whom an empty date removes nothing else (the council page patches the council) passes `finishedDatesOnly`: only a finished day (`isDateOnlyKey` in `dateOnly.ts`, counted, not parsed) or `""` is handed over, half-typed text stays in the field, a day arriving from elsewhere does not overwrite it, and leaving the field shows what is saved.
- react-day-picker v9: the selected state sits on the cell (`.rdp-selected`), the visible circle is its child button. Style `.rdp-selected .rdp-day_button`; the legacy `.rdp-day_selected` alone does not reach it. Check computed colours in dark mode (`PreachCalendar.tsx` and `DatePickerField.tsx` both carry the rule).

Calendar analytics drill-down tiles (`AnalyticsSection.tsx`, both testament grids and monthly activity) are native `type="button"` controls, disabled when the count is zero. Let the browser activate Enter and Space; the shared modal layer owns focus and return. Opening on a div's keydown can move focus to Close before the same Enter's keypress, immediately closing the new window. Keep full-key-sequence regressions and real browser checks; keydown-only dispatch misses that failure. Use block spans for the former tile layout inside buttons.

Book distribution and its sermon lists share `extractBookIdsFromVerse` in `calendarAnalytics.ts`. It reads actual places through `referencesInText` first and preserves calendar-only word forms only outside those spans; duplicate references in one sermon still count that sermon once per book and preach date. Check both counts and drill-down lists, including a mixed canonical/legacy field, instead of changing either consumer independently.

## Traps

- A text date field that passes keystrokes to a caller that saves each change stores every prefix. Do not turn `finishedDatesOnly` on for a form: the field then rolls a half-typed date back on blur and the form submits the old day instead of refusing. Group editing instead uses `useGroupMeetingEditor`: date, location and audience share one durable `useDataForm` stage and one750ms autosave. Partial/impossible input cancels submission and remains recoverable; completing or clearing the date schedules the combined stage, including navigation/pagehide flush. Clearing uses `editFirstGroupMeeting` and keeps the same meeting, details and siblings. `groupMeetingDay` is the common reader for card/calendar/range/dashboard; it rejects partial/impossible legacy values without rewriting them. The form permits a detail-only edit with an unchanged legacy date by comparing meeting identity and exact raw date to `initialData` (the preceding saved intent or baseline), never to the editable/recovered stage. Normalization preserves that unchanged raw value. Server admission allows empty/complete dates and preserves unchanged legacy values when another field changes (BUG-20261003-group-meeting-date-saved-while-typing and BUG-20261003-group-meeting-clear-drops-place).

A meeting identity is not a calendar day or proof that the meeting was conducted. `GroupMeetingDate` has no per-meeting completion flag; a future or cleared date keeps the same record. The shared `SeriesDetailView` therefore sums the meeting arrays of group members and uses the same neutral meeting caption as `SeriesItemCard`; an empty group contributes zero. Do not count groups with nonempty arrays, infer completion from elapsed days, or drop undated records. Keep the existing unknown-read total (`—`) until contributing sources are known. Local phase138 covers the aggregate, three real dictionaries, cleared-date versus explicit removal, and future/undated controls.

## Why

- 2026-02-10: preach dates drifted a day between markers, list and analytics — hence one date form and one pipeline.
- The calendar first carried sermons and groups as two types with a `kind === 'sermon' ? … : …` in every view; a third kind would have nested every ternary, so the sources became one entry shape.
- 2026-05-10: the selected day vanished in dark mode because only the v8 class was styled.
- 2026-10-03: council dates moved off the native `input type="date"` (it ignored a Monday week start); the move showed that the text field handed over every keystroke. Making that the field's default broke forms and drafts, and turning it on for the group page deleted meetings mid-typing, so it became the opt-in `finishedDatesOnly` with one caller.
